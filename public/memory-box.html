GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What exactly is a Memory Box?

A private place to keep video messages for the people you love, and to choose when each one is delivered.

Can I choose when it arrives?

Yes. Pick a date, a life moment like a wedding or graduation, or "someday," which means a message delivered after you've passed. We confirm the moment carefully before anything is released.

Who can see my messages?

Only the people you choose, only when you choose. Sealed messages stay private until delivery.

What does it cost?

Start free with 5GB. If you need more room, Memory Box+ is $4.99 a month for 500GB.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You can re-record, reschedule, or remove a message whenever you like.
