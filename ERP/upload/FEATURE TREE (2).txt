FEATURE TREE  v3   (updated 2026-11-02, reconciled against s screenshots)
=====================================================================
Changes from v2
  * Finance regrouped to five groups (Accounting, Business Tax, Expenses & Bills,
    Sales & Get Paid, Customer Hub). Every group starts with Overview.
  * Create menu regrouped by counterparty: Customers | Vendors & suppliers | Team | Other,
    plus two commerce columns (Catalog & marketing, Support)
  * Business view / Accountant view toggle (ERP books sidebar becomes Accountant view)
  * Product drawer gets Sales / Purchasing / Accounting sections (income + COGS mapping)
  * Universal footer grammar, split-button "New transaction" menus, empty states with Import
  * Party list surfaces in three places (Customer Hub, Expenses & Bills, Catalog) from one data list
  * Added: Business Tax group, Lists & Tools (gear menu), setup checklist, receipts inbox
    with forwarding address, recurring template types, list customizer, reconcile setup,
    contractor invite modal, unified transaction lists, per-domain pickable create actions
  * Cleanup: Customers page columns swap Pets/Appointments for commerce columns
  * Skipped on purpose: Mileage, Lending, Payroll (revisit later)
Legend:  [D] document  [R] right drawer  [M] modal  [W] workspace/wizard  [P] full page
         [inline] = page-local, no shared component yet   (P1/P2/P3) = build priority
