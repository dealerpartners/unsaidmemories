info Product and design guidelines

Semi-transparencies and gradients may not print as shown

Please note: DTF prints have a white underbase and cannot offer full transparency. When gradients and semi-transparencies are used in a design, the printed outcome will differ from what is seen in the mockups.

Design limitations (Single-color prints)

The process used for creating single-color print products will impact the final look of colors and designs. Before creating this product, please review the guide here.

Multiple decoration methods

DTG decoration method

Using this technique, pigmented water-based inks are applied to the garment’s surface and are absorbed by the product’s fibers and can accommodate precise and detailed designs.

Embroidery decoration method

We recommend using designs with full shapes that are spaced from each other (instead of overlapping or being close together).

Your design should NOT contain:

  • Photos
  • Gradients
  • Small details (< 0.05" (1.3 mm))
  • Small text (< 0.25" (6.4 mm))
  • Small negative spaces
  • Transparency around the edges

Also, please only use predefined embroidery thread colors (up to 6 from 15 available). For more precise designs, please use our downloadable templates.

Important: Products that don't meet the requirements above may be automatically changed or rejected. See the embroidery guide here.
