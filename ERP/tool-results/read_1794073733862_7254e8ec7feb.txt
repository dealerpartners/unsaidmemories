     1→# Unified Platform — Component Map & System Logic
     2→
     3→Commerce + Design + Accounting, one connected system. This doc defines, for every module: the primary entity, which surface opens for each action (drawer / modal / page / workspace / studio), table columns and sorts, right-click menus, pills, and what rides along from other modules.

     6→
     7→## PART 1 — System Logic (the rules every module obeys)
     8→
     9→### 1.1 The five shared entities
    10→
    11→Everything in the Create Actions list collapses into five entity families. This is what makes the platform feel integrated: modules are *views* over shared entities, not separate databases.
    12→
    13→| Entity | What it is | Roles / Types | Used by |
    14→|---|---|---|---|
    15→| **Party** | One person/company record | Customer, Vendor, Supplier, Contractor, Seller, Lead (a party can hold many roles) | Sales, Expenses, CRM, Orders, Support, Tax (1099), Marketplace |
    16→| **Item** | Anything sellable/buyable | Product, Service, Blank, Bundle, Design-linked product, Gift card, Subscription | Catalog, Studio, Invoices, Bills, PO, Orders, Inventory, Channels |
    17→| **Document** | Any multi-line transaction | Invoice, Estimate, Sales order, Sales receipt, Credit memo, Bill, Expense, Check, PO, Vendor credit, RMA, Journal entry | Sales, Expenses, Orders, Purchasing, Returns, Accounting |
    18→| **Money movement** | Cash actually moving | Payment, Payment link, Deposit, Transfer, Settlement/Payout, Refund | Sales, Bills, Banking, Reconcile, Stripe/Shopify |
    19→| **Account** | Ledger node | Chart of Accounts, Bank, Credit card, Loan, Line of credit | Accounting, every Document line, Reconcile, Tax, Reports |
    20→
    21→**Rule:** "Add vendor" and "Add supplier" and "Add customer" are the **same drawer** (Party drawer) with a different default role pill. Same for "Add contractor" (role = Contractor, plus W-9 invite section). Never build four forms.
    22→
    23→**Rule:** Every Document is rendered by **one Document Engine** (header → lines → totals → attachments/memo → status → activity). Invoice, Bill, PO, Sales order, Estimate, Credit memo, Vendor credit differ only by *config* (party role, line columns, status machine, posting rules). Build once.
    24→
    25→### 1.2 Surface rules — which container for which job
    26→
    27→| Surface | Use when | Examples | Escape hatch |
    28→|---|---|---|---|
    29→| **Pill / popover (peek)** | Read-only glance, zero commitment | Hover a customer pill → balance, last order, open tickets | Click → open drawer; ⌘-click → new tab |
    30→| **Modal** | Single decision, ≤5 fields, result is immediate | Record payment, Void, Refund, Mark shipped, Adjust stock, Transfer, Send, Remind, Approve/Decline return, Archive | None (small by design) |
    31→| **Drawer (right, 480px; 720px wide mode)** | Create/edit a *record* while staying on the list | Party, Product, Account, Category, Task, Note, Ticket, Banner, Popup, Discount code, Collection | **Make full page** (already in your Bills create menu) |
    32→| **Document page (full)** | Multi-line transactions | Invoice, Bill, PO, Sales order, Estimate, Credit memo, Journal entry | Print/PDF/Email views as tabs inside the page |
    33→| **Workspace** | Many records, one batch outcome | Pay bills, Receive payment, Reconcile, Batch slips & labels, Cycle count, Upload multiple bills | Saves as draft batch |
    34→| **Studio** | Visual canvas | Design, Collection canvas, Product mockup | "Back to record" returns to originating drawer |
    35→| **Command palette (⌘K)** | Jump or create anywhere | "New invoice for Acme", "Bill from Printful" | Same Create menu, searchable |
    36→
    37→**Escalation ladder:** Peek → Modal → Drawer → Full page. A user can always promote upward without losing input (drawer → full page keeps state).

    39→### 1.3 The Context Envelope (this is the integration engine)
    40→
    41→Every surface carries an invisible envelope. Creating something from inside a record **prefills from that record**; creating from the global Create menu inherits only the **active tab's** envelope.
    42→
    43→```
    44→envelope = {
    45→  origin_module, origin_record_id, origin_tab_id,
    46→  party_id, order_id, item_id, design_id, account_id,
    47→  channel_id, currency, date_range, return_to
    48→}
    49→```
    50→
    51→Examples:
    52→- On **Order #1042** → Create → Invoice: party, lines, channel, and `order_id` are prefilled and linked.
    53→- On **Vendor: Printful** → Create → Bill: vendor, terms, default expense category prefilled; open POs from that vendor show as a "Match PO" suggestion.
    54→- On a **Design** in Studio → Create → Product: design attached, blank picker opens, margins pre-calculated from supplier cost.
    55→- On **Bank transaction** (Reconcile) → Create → Expense: amount, date, paid-from prefilled; "Match" and "Create" are siblings.
    56→
    57→`return_to` means closing any drawer/modal/page returns to the exact scroll position and tab it came from.

   133→### 1.9 Scoped Workspaces → Global Hubs (the Push pattern)
   134→
   135→Catalog, Contacts/CRM, Support and Marketing each exist **twice on purpose**: once as a **scoped view** inside the area where the work happens, and once as the **global hub** that is the system of record. This is how a user never has to leave what they're doing to get or create what they need.
   136→
   137→| Scoped view (where you work) | Lives in | Global hub (system of record) | Push action | Pull action |
   138→|---|---|---|---|---|
   139→| **My Catalog** | Design Studio / My products | **Global Catalog** | **Publish** | Add a blank/product from global into My Catalog |
   140→| **Contacts** | Finance (Sales, Expenses, Accounting) | **Global Contacts / CRM** | **Create and push** | **Search global → Use here** |
   141→| **Support** | Record context (Orders, Sales, Customers) | **Global Support** (Inbox & Tickets) | Create ticket here → lands in global queue | Link an existing global ticket |
   142→| **Marketing** | Studio / Catalog / Product context | **Global Marketing** | Promote here → lands in global calendar/queue | Pull from global library/campaigns |
   143→
   144→**Why two:** The designer cannot bounce to the global catalog mid-design. The user in Finance should not have to open CRM to bill someone. Scoped = fast, in context, only what you need. Global = governed, searchable, shared across the whole platform.
   145→
   146→**Rules**
   147→
   148→1. **One record identity.** A scoped view is a lens (or staging copy) linked to a single global ID. Pushing never makes a second record.
   149→2. **Two entry flows, same destination.**
   150→   - *Create → push:* new contact/product made in scope, then promoted to global.
   151→   - *Search → link:* user searches the global hub from the scoped view and attaches the existing record. Search always runs **before** create ("Does this already exist?").
   152→3. **Dedupe on push.** Match keys: Contact = email / tax ID / name + address; Product = SKU / design ID; Ticket = requester + related record. On a match the push modal offers **Link to existing · Merge · Create anyway**.
   153→4. **State pill on every scoped row:** `Local only` → `Pushed` → `Live/Global`. Catalog adds `Draft → Published → Syncing to channels`.
   154→5. **Provenance:** global records carry a source pill (`Created in Studio`, `Created in Finance`) so context is never lost after the push.
   155→6. **Visibility:** a scoped item is visible to the creator/team until pushed; global visibility follows Roles & permissions. Pushing is its own permission (e.g. Publish to catalog).
   156→7. **Edit from either view, with an "existing record" warning.** The user is never sent to the other view to make a change. Because there is only one record underneath (rule 1), an edit made in the scoped view or the global hub writes to the same record and shows up in both immediately. The safeguard is a warning on entering edit, not a redirect.
   157→
   158→   **Warning tiers (shown when edit opens, in the drawer/page header):**
   159→
   160→   | Tier | Applies to | What the user sees |
   161→   |---|---|---|
   162→   | **1 — Notice** | Local only / not yet pushed | No warning needed |
   163→   | **2 — Existing record banner** | Pushed/Global record, edited from anywhere | Banner: *"You're editing an existing record shared with Global Catalog."* + impact line (e.g. *Used in 14 products · 3 open invoices · 2 channels*) + **View where used**. Non-blocking. |
   164→   | **3 — Confirm on save** | Edit changes a field with downstream effect (price, SKU, email, tax ID, terms, default account) | Save opens a small modal with a before → after summary of what propagates; options **Save** · **Save and apply to open drafts** · Cancel |
   165→   | **4 — Locked** | Posted financial documents, reconciled transactions | Fields locked; offer **Void**, **Adjust**, or **Create credit memo** instead of edit |
   166→
   167→   **Safeguards that make edit-anywhere safe:**
   168→   - **Source pill in the activity log:** every edit records *edited from My Catalog / Studio / Finance / Global*, who, and the before → after values.
   169→   - **Concurrency:** soft lock banner (*"Maria is editing this"*) and a version check on save; on conflict show a field-level diff to keep mine, keep theirs, or merge.
   170→   - **Permissions still apply:** edit rights follow Roles and permissions; fields like cost, margin and bank details stay masked and read-only without the right role.
   171→   - **Undo:** a toast with **Undo** after save for Tier 2 edits, plus Activity → Restore previous value.
   172→   - **Same warning component everywhere:** one Existing Record Banner component, parameterized by record type and impact counts, used by Party, Item, Ticket, Campaign and Account drawers.
   173→8. **Create menu:** actions in a scoped context default to creating locally with **Push to global** pre-checked in the drawer footer (`Save` · `Save and push`).
