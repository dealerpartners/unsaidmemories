   675→## PART 3.5 — Domain Structure & the Undefined Modules
   676→
   677→### A. The test: standalone module, sub-area, or setting?
   678→
   679→A thing earns its own **standalone module** only if it passes at least 4 of 5:
   680→
   681→1. Own primary entity with its own lifecycle
   682→2. Own list view people live in
   683→3. Own permission role (someone's job is this)
   684→4. Used by 3+ other modules
   685→5. Own ledger or compliance impact
   686→
   687→Otherwise it is a **sub-area** of a domain parent, or an **engine/setting** that other screens call.
   688→
   689→| Candidate | Entity | Own list | Own role | Used by | Ledger/compliance | Verdict |
   690→|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   691→| **Inventory** | Stock record + stock ledger | Yes | Warehouse/ops | Catalog, Orders, PO, Returns, Sales | Asset + COGS | **Standalone page** under Operations |
   692→| **Payroll** | Employee, Pay run, Pay stub | Yes | Payroll admin (restricted) | Accounting, Tax, Time, Reports | Heavy | **Standalone page** under People |
   693→| **Time** | Time entry | Weekly grid only | No | Payroll, Invoices, Tasks | Indirect | **Collapse** into People (feature, not a module) |
   694→| **Sales tax** | Rate, jurisdiction, filing | Filings only | Finance | Invoices, Orders, Items, Parties, Reports | Liability account | **Engine in Settings** + sub-area of Business Tax |
   695→
   696→### B. Catalog vs Inventory vs POD (the fix)
   697→
   698→Catalog, Inventory and Purchasing are three layers over the same Item, not three competing modules.
   699→
   700→| Layer | Question it answers | Owns |
   701→|---|---|---|
   702→| **Catalog** | *What is this and where is it sold?* | Definition, price, variants, **designs**, blanks, supplier link, channels, publish state |
   703→| **Inventory** | *What do I physically have?* | On hand, reserved, available, locations, cost layers, counts, transfers, adjustments, stock history |
   704→| **Purchasing** | *How does supply arrive?* | Purchase orders, receiving, supplier cost |
   705→
   706→Every Item gets a **Fulfillment type**, set in the Item drawer. It decides which tabs, pills and PO behavior the item has:
   707→
   708→| Fulfillment type | Example | Tracks stock? | Availability comes from | PO type | Pill |
   709→|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   710→| **Stock** | In-house goods, wholesale | **Yes** | Inventory on-hand | **Replenishment PO** (manual or reorder-point) | `42 in stock` |
   711→| **POD (print on demand)** | Design on a blank | No | Supplier / blank availability | **Fulfillment PO**, auto-created per order | `Made on demand` |
   712→| **Supplier-fulfilled (dropship)** | Supplier ships direct | No | Supplier feed | **Fulfillment PO**, auto-created per order | `Ships from supplier` |
   713→| **Digital / Service** | Download, hourly service | No | Always available | None | none |
   714→| **Bundle** | Tee + hat set | Derived | Lowest component availability | Per component | `Bundle` |
   715→
   716→**Consequences**
   717→- **Catalog owns POD completely:** Design → Product → Blank → Supplier. A POD product has no stock number, so the Inventory tab, Adjust stock and Cycle count never appear for it.
   718→- **Inventory is only for real stock.** Its list shows only items with *Track inventory = on*.
   719→- **Two PO types.** Replenishment PO is a manual or reorder-triggered buy for stock. Fulfillment PO is generated from an Order line and is linked to that order. Same Document Engine, different config.
   720→- **Blanks:** if the supplier holds them, a Blank is a Catalog record with supplier availability. If *you* buy and hold blanks (in-house printing), the Blank becomes an Inventory item (raw material), which later needs a simple production/BOM step. Flag this as a future decision.
   721→- **The stock pill on a product row** is replaced by the fulfillment pill for POD items.
   722→
   723→### C. Recommended domain parents
   724→
   725→Each parent is a nav group containing sub-areas. Each sub-area keeps its own list and Create actions.
   726→
   727→| Domain parent | Contains |
   728→|---|---|
   729→| **Finance** | Accounting · Sales · Expenses & Bills · **Business Tax** (Income tax · **Sales tax** · **Payroll tax** · 1099s) |
   730→| **People** | **Employees** · Contractors · **Time** · **Payroll** |
   731→| **Operations** | Orders · Fulfillment · Purchasing · **Inventory** · Returns · Shipping |
   732→| **Commerce** (Catalog & Design) | Studio + My Catalog → Global Catalog · Channels |
   733→| **Growth** | CRM · Marketing · Social · Ads |
   734→| **Support** | Inbox & Tickets · Queues · SLA · Trust & Safety |
   735→| **Reports / Settings** | Unchanged |
   736→
   737→Notes on the moves:
   738→- **Contractors** are a People sub-area *and* keep a scoped view inside Expenses & Bills that pushes to People, the same pattern as section 1.9.
   739→- **Employees** are a Party role, so they use the same Party drawer with Pay, Tax and Bank sections (masked and restricted).
   740→- **Sales tax and Payroll tax** live under Business Tax because they are filings and liabilities, while the *engines* that feed them live elsewhere (Settings and Payroll).
   741→
   742→### D. Definitions for the four
   743→
   744→**Payroll** (People)
   745→- Entities: Employee (Party role), Pay schedule, Pay run, Pay stub, Tax forms (W-2, 941).
   746→- **Add employee:** Party drawer with Pay (salary/hourly, schedule), Tax (W-4, state), Bank (direct deposit), and an onboarding invite.
   747→- **Run payroll:** Workspace. Period → hours/salary (hours pulled from Time) → review gross / deductions / taxes / net → approve → pay.
   748→- Pay run list columns: Period · Pay date · Employees · Gross · Taxes · Net · Status pill (Draft / Approved / Paid) · ⋯
   749→- Posts journal entries automatically (wages expense, tax liabilities, bank). Rides along into Business Tax → Payroll tax and Reports.
   750→- **Decision to make:** build vs integrate. Payroll is compliance-heavy (filings, multi-state tax). Define the entity, surfaces and ledger posting now, and consider a provider integration behind the same screens.
   751→
   752→**Time** (People)
   753→- **Time entry:** modal or weekly grid. Fields: Person, Date, Hours, Related (Customer / Task / Order), Billable toggle, Rate, Note.
   754→- Rides along: Invoice has **Add billable time** on its line picker; Payroll pulls approved hours; Tasks can start a timer.
   755→
   756→**Sales tax** (Business Tax + Settings engine)
   757→- **Engine (Settings):** Rates and jurisdictions, tax categories, nexus rules.
   758→- **Item drawer:** Tax category field. **Party drawer:** Tax exempt toggle + certificate upload.
   759→- **Document Engine:** per-line tax calculation and a tax summary in the totals block.
   760→- **Business Tax → Sales tax:** liability by jurisdiction · Filing periods · **Pay sales tax** (workspace, like Pay bills) · Nexus alert pills.
   761→- Orders from channels arrive with marketplace-collected tax flagged, so it is excluded from your liability.
   762→
   763→**Inventory** (Operations)
   764→- Defined in Module: Purchasing & Inventory, now restricted to *Stock* items. Add: **Locations** (warehouses/bins) and a **Stock ledger** (every movement with source pill: PO receipt, order, adjustment, transfer, return).

   768→## PART 3.6 — POS, Gift Cards, Banking & Cards
   769→
   770→### A. POS (brick-and-mortar)
   771→
   772→**Principle:** POS is a **channel + a device surface**, not a separate system. A store sale is a normal Order/Sales receipt with a store channel pill, so Inventory, Accounting, CRM, Returns and Support all work unchanged.
   773→
   774→**Entities:** Location (store) · Register · Shift (drawer session) · Sale · Tender (card, cash, gift card, split) · Receipt · Hardware (reader, receipt printer, cash drawer, scanner)
   775→
   776→| Surface | Type | Contents |
   777→|---|---|---|
   778→| **POS app** | Full-screen, touch-first, offline-capable | Product grid + search + barcode scan → cart → attach customer (Party picker, optional, with `+ Add`) → discounts → tender → receipt (print/email/SMS) |
   779→| Open shift | Modal | Register, starting cash |
   780→| Close shift | Modal → count sheet | Counted cash vs expected, over/short, card totals, tips, **Z report** |
   781→| Cash in / out | Modal | Amount, reason (paid-out, petty cash) |
   782→| Park / resume sale | Inline | Held carts per register |
   783→| Refund / exchange | Modal | Scan or search receipt, select lines, restock toggle, refund to original tender or store credit |
   784→| Redeem / sell gift card | Modal | See 3.6 B |
   785→| Manager override | PIN modal | Required for discounts above threshold, voids, refunds, price override |
   786→
   787→**Back-office views:** Locations list (Name · Address · Registers · Open shift pill · Today's sales · Stock alerts) · Shifts list (Register · Cashier · Opened · Closed · Expected · Counted · Variance pill) · POS sales appear in Orders and Sales with a **Channel pill** `Store: Downtown`.
   788→
   789→**Rides along**
   790→- **Inventory:** stock is tracked per location; sales decrement the selling location; transfers between stores and warehouse; optional buy-online-pickup-in-store.
   791→- **POD items in store:** sold as an *order for later* (ship or pickup), never as instant stock.
   792→- **Accounting:** Z report posts a journal entry (sales by tender, tax, tips, cash over/short, gift card liability); card settlement reconciles to payouts in Bank transactions.
   793→- **Sales tax:** location-based rates and exemptions.
   794→- **CRM:** attached customer gets the sale on their profile; walk-up sales stay anonymous.
   795→- **Support:** receipt lookup creates a ticket or RMA with the sale pre-linked.
   796→- **Permissions:** Cashier, Shift lead, Manager roles; override rules live in Settings → Approvals and limits.
   797→- **Offline mode:** queue sales and card-present fallbacks, sync on reconnect, and define oversell conflict rules before launch.
   798→
   799→### B. Gift cards
   800→
   801→**Treat gift cards as your own ledger entity.** A gift card sale is a **liability**, not revenue; revenue is recognized when the card is redeemed.
   802→
   803→**Entities:** Gift card (code, initial value, balance, currency, issued to, expiry, status) · Gift card transaction (issue, redeem, reload, refund, adjust, expire)
   804→
   805→| Action | Surface | Notes |
   806→|---|---|---|
   807→| Sell gift card | Item type = Gift card (digital or physical) | Sold online, in POS, or on an invoice; card is created on payment |
   808→| Issue manually | **Drawer** | Amount, recipient, message, delivery (email / print), expiry |
   809→| Check balance | Popover / modal | Enter or scan code |
   810→| Redeem | Tender at checkout (online + POS) | Supports partial redemption and split tender |
   811→| Reload / Adjust / Disable | Modal | Reason required, logged |
   812→| Refund to gift card | Option in Refund modal | Or to store credit |
   813→
   814→**List columns:** ☐ · Code (masked) · Recipient · Purchaser (pill) · Issued · Initial · Balance · Expires · Status pill (Active / Partially used / Redeemed / Expired / Disabled) · Source (Online / Store / Manual) · ⋯
   815→**Right-click:** Open · Email card · Print · Reload · Adjust · **Create from this ▸** Ticket · Note · Disable
   816→**Detail:** Ledger of every movement with source pill (Order #, Register, Manual).
   817→**Accounting:** Gift card liability account; unredeemed-balance reports. Expiry, breakage and unclaimed-property rules vary by jurisdiction, so have a lawyer or accountant set the policy.
   818→**Store credit** (from credit memos and returns) uses the same ledger with a different type.
   819→
   820→### C. Banking & Cards (Stripe Connect + Treasury + Issuing)
   821→
   822→**Model:** Merchants are Connect connected accounts. Treasury for platforms provides a financial account per merchant; Issuing provides debit cards drawn on that account. Payouts from Stripe settle into the financial account.
   823→
   824→**Surfaces (Finance → Banking, merchant-facing)**
   825→
   826→| Surface | Type | Contents |
   827→|---|---|---|
   828→| Financial account | Page | Balance, available, pending, recent movement |
   829→| Move money | Modal | Transfer to / from linked bank, ACH out, payout sweep rules |
   830→| Cards list | List | Card · Holder (Party/Team pill) · Virtual/Physical · Status · Limit · Spent this period · ⋯ |
   831→| Issue card | **Drawer** | Holder, type, spend limit, allowed merchant categories, shipping, approval rules |
   832→| Card transactions | Feed in Bank transactions | Auto-create **Expense** with receipt-capture prompt; match to Bill; categorize via Rules |
   833→| Pay bills from financial account | Option in Pay bills workspace | Uses ACH details on the Vendor |
   834→| Onboarding / KYC-KYB | Drawer or embedded component | Collect and verify business info before enabling Banking |
   835→
   836→**Rides along:** Card spend limits tie to **Approvals and limits** · Settlement posts payouts into the financial account · Reconcile treats the financial account as a bank · Expenses and Business tax read card spend.
   837→
   838→**Compliance surfaces you must design (they land in Support):** a regulated **Complaints and Disputes queue** with recordkeeping, plus disclosures and customer-service access inside the Banking UI.

   842→## PART 3.7 — Support, Deepened
   843→
   844→Support is the module most likely to feel bolted on, so it is designed here as a **cross-module resolution layer**: an agent resolves the problem *from the ticket*, without leaving it.
   845→
   846→### A. Who Support serves (queues)
   847→
   848→| Queue | Requester | Examples |
   849→|---|---|---|
   850→| **Customer** | Buyers | Order issues, returns, payment questions, product questions |
   851→| **Seller** | Marketplace sellers | Onboarding, payouts, listing issues, compliance |
   852→| **Attention** | Anyone | SLA at risk, escalated, manual-push failures |
   853→| **Disputes and Complaints** | Buyers and merchants | Payment disputes/chargebacks (links to Sales → Disputes) and regulated Banking/Card complaints |
   854→| **Internal** (optional) | Your team | Platform bugs, requests |
   855→
   856→### B. Ticket model
   857→
   858→**Ticket type** drives fields, sidebar cards and available actions: Question · Order issue · Return · Payment / dispute · Billing · Complaint · Seller onboarding · Bug.
   859→**Fields:** Subject · Requester (Party pill) · Type · Channel · Priority · Status (New / Open / Pending / On hold / Solved / Closed) · Assignee · Queue · Tags · **Related records** (Order, Invoice, RMA, Product, Design, Shipment, Gift card).
   860→

   872→### D. Inbound sources and automation
   873→
   874→- **Sources:** email, chat, social DMs, marketplace messages (Etsy, Amazon, etc.), web forms, **Report a problem… on an order**, POS receipt lookup, Banking complaint form.
   875→- **Automation (trigger → condition → action):** *Trigger:* ticket created, RMA created, payment failed, order delayed, SLA warning. *Action:* assign, route to queue, set priority, tag, auto-reply, create task, notify.
   876→- **Macros** with merge fields (customer, order, tracking).
   877→- **SLA policies:** first response and resolution targets by queue, priority and customer tier; timers pause on Pending; respects Office hours; breach raises an Alert pill and a task.
   878→
   879→### E. Self-service and knowledge
   880→
   881→- **Help center / customer portal:** order status, tracking, self-serve returns (creates an RMA), past tickets, gift card balance. This is the deflection layer.
   882→- **Knowledge base:** articles, docs, policies; suggested in the composer and on the contact form; **Create article from ticket**; article feedback.
   883→- **CSAT:** survey on solve; QA review sampling.
   884→
   885→### F. Feedback loops into the rest of the platform
   886→
   887→| Signal | Lands in |
   888→|---|---|
   889→| High issue or return rate per product | Catalog **alert pill** on the product + task |
   890→| Supplier defect | Vendor credit / supplier claim |
   891→| Fulfillment failures | Orders → Needs attention |
   892→| Seller problems | Seller detail + Onboarding queue |
   893→| Complaint volume by reason | Reports → Support (reason, first response, resolution, backlog, CSAT) |
   894→
   895→**Permissions:** Agent, Lead, Admin. Sensitive fields (card, bank, tax IDs) masked in conversations. Regulated complaints and disputes are retained with full audit history.
